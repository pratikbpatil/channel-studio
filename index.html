Channel Studio

A private desktop tool for publishing our own animated bedtime stories to YouTube.

Channel Studio runs on one Mac. It turns our own original stories into animated videos and uploads them to our own YouTube channels (for example, Moonbrook Tales) as private videos with a scheduled publish time.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to:

  • upload our videos, thumbnails and captions,
  • add videos to our own playlists,
  • set or change a video's scheduled publish time, and
  • read back our own videos' status to confirm they were scheduled correctly.

It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in, and only with their own channels.
