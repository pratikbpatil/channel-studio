Channel Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Channel Studio is a private desktop application that the channel owner runs on their own Mac to upload and schedule their own videos on YouTube. It uses YouTube API Services. By using Channel Studio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Channel Studio uses

  • Google sign-in token: created when you sign in with Google. It lets the app act on your YouTube channel.
  • Your channel's information: the channel name and ID, plus the title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time of the videos the app uploads.
  • Your own files: the stories, videos, thumbnails and captions you create in the app.

How it is used

The data is used only to upload your videos, thumbnails and captions to your channel, add them to your playlists, set or change their scheduled publish time, and read their status back to confirm the upload worked. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain, and other data is kept in a local database and folders on that Mac. Channel Studio has no server of its own. It does not send your data to anyone except Google, through the YouTube API, when it carries out the actions above.

Sharing

Channel Studio does not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party, and it does not use it for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data and revoking access

You can remove Channel Studio's access at any time on the Google security settings page. Removing a channel from Channel Studio deletes its stored token from the Keychain, and deleting the app's folder removes all its local data.
